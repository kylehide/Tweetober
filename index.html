      Code of conduct

      The Code

      Rules and Regulations

      • The Tweetober tally drops every 5 days.
      • Each Tweetobie must count their daily tweets and report them to the Committee in the Ledger.
      • Only original tweets count.
      • Retweets do not count.
      • Quote tweets count, but you must add something of substance to the quoted tweet. No Tweetdecking.
      • Replies do not count.
      • Threads count. You may reply to yourself.
      • Deleted tweets do not count.

      The Honor Code

      Tweetober is a noble pursuit, ruled by chivalry and honor. I will only post quality tweets. I will not try to beat the system. I tweet good and true, for the love of the game.

      May the best tweeter win.

      Tweetober is a friendly competition among friends.
